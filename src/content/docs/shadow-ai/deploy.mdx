Arcjet Discovery finds shadow AI with an endpoint agent that runs on your
organization's managed macOS and Windows devices. You deploy the agent with the
device management (MDM) tool that you already use. Each device then reports
connections to AI services to your Arcjet project.

The agent runs in the background as a system service, so it keeps reporting
without anyone opening an app. For more information about what it reports, see <Link.Page href="/shadow-ai/data-collected">What Discovery collects</Link.Page>.

## Before you begin

Make sure that you have the following:

- Administrator access to your Arcjet team.
- Devices enrolled in your MDM tool, and permission to deploy software to
  them.

Tell the people whose devices you deploy to that the agent records which AI
services and apps they use.

## Deploy the agent

To deploy the agent to your devices, do the following:

1. In the [Arcjet Console](https://console.arcjet.com), open your project and
   select the **Discovery** tab.
2. Open the deployment steps, and then create an enrollment token. The token
   connects devices to your project. Copy the token when it appears, because
   the Console shows it only once.
3. Download the agent from the Console.
4. In your MDM tool, deploy the agent and the enrollment token to your devices
   by following the steps in the Console. Start with a small group of devices.
5. In the **Discovery** tab, open **Devices** and confirm that each device is
   reporting.

Treat the enrollment token like a password. Deliver it to devices only through
your MDM tool.

The agent needs system-level permissions to observe network connections. When
your MDM tool installs the agent, it grants those permissions, so people don't
need to approve anything on their device.

## Confirm that devices are reporting

The **Devices** view lists each enrolled device and shows whether it's
reporting. A device's first AI activity appears after someone on it uses an AI
app or opens an AI service in a browser. Activity then appears in the
**People**, **Devices**, and **Providers** views.

After the first devices report, deploy the agent to the rest of your fleet.

## Update or remove the agent

To update the agent, deploy the new version with your MDM tool.

To stop a device from reporting, revoke its access from **Devices** in the
Console. A revoked device needs a new enrollment token before it can report
again. To uninstall the agent, remove it with your MDM tool.
