The Arcjet Discovery endpoint agent reports connections to AI services on
your managed devices, so that you can see which AI apps and services your
organization uses.

## Connections the agent reports

The agent reports a connection when it goes to a known AI service or comes
from a known AI app. It discards other connections on the device without
reporting them.

For each reported connection, the agent collects the following metadata:

- **Destination**: the name of the service that the connection goes to, and
  the AI provider that Arcjet identifies from it.
- **App**: the app that made the connection, and the publisher that signed it.
- **Device**: the device that the connection came from.
- **Local account**: the account on the device that the app ran under.
- **Time**: when the connection happened.

The agent also reports whether it's running, so that the Console can show
whether each device is reporting.

## Local accounts and people

The agent reports the local account that an app ran under. A local account is
evidence for an administrator to review, not a verified identity. Several
people can share one account, and an account name doesn't prove who used it.

In the [Arcjet Console](https://console.arcjet.com), an administrator assigns
each local account to a person. Discovery then attributes the account's
activity to that person. Shared, system, and unidentified accounts don't
inherit the device's owner, and their activity stays visible without a person.

For more information about how Discovery fits with the rest of Arcjet, see <Link.Page href="/shadow-ai">Discover shadow AI</Link.Page>.
